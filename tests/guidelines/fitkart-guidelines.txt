FitKart Communication Guidelines

FitKart is a fictional online store for home fitness equipment in India.

Voice
- Motivating and positive, never body-shaming. Never comment on people's weight or looks.
- Address the reader as "you". Use simple English.
- Use exclamation marks freely to keep energy high!!
- Use at most one exclamation mark per post.

Terminology
- Say "home gym", not "exercise machine".
- Write "FitKart" (capital F and K). Not "Fitkart" or "Fit Kart".
- Say "budget-friendly", never "cheap".

Offers and pricing
- Every discount must show the end date.
- Always mention "free delivery" in every advertisement.
- Never promise free delivery; delivery charges depend on pincode.
- Do not promise weight-loss results or guaranteed outcomes.

Approved facts
- 2 lakh+ orders delivered (Ops dashboard, Sep 2026, valid till Sep 2027)
- 7-day easy returns (Returns policy, valid till Dec 2027)

Brand colours: Energy Orange #FF6B1A, Deep Charcoal #1F2328, Pure White #FFFFFF.

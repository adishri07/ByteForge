# BrightPath Learning — Brand Guidelines (demo brand)

*BrightPath Learning is a fictional online learning app used to demonstrate BrandProof. Owner: Member 6.*

## Who we are
BrightPath helps learners across India build job-ready skills through short video lessons, live doubt-solving and certificates.

## Voice and tone
- **R-01** Friendly, encouraging and respectful — never pushy, fear-based or sarcastic.
- **R-02** Simple, clear English. No slang ("lit", "dope", "bro").
- **R-03** Talk about what learners gain and how we support them — not about beating other platforms.
- **R-11** No ALL CAPS words. **R-12** One exclamation mark at most. **R-13** Max 2 emojis per post.

## Words we use
- **R-04** "learners", never "students" or "clients".
- **R-06** The name is always "BrightPath".
- **R-07** "affordable", never "cheap".
- **R-05** We never name competitors.

## Claims
- **R-08** Only numbers from the Approved Facts list.
- **R-09** Never promise guaranteed or 100% results, jobs or marks.
- **R-10** No "#1", "best" or "fastest" without an approved fact.

## Readability and accessibility
- **R-14** Sentences under 30 words.
- **R-15** Don't rely on colour alone ("click the green button").
